Watch Drill Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Watch Drill is an unofficial promotional study aid. It is not affiliated with the Los Angeles Police Department or the City of Los Angeles Personnel Department.

What we collect

The app does not create an account and does not upload your answers to us.

Quiz results (correct, incorrect, and review schedule) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing site data deletes that information.

Links

If you open a public source link (bibliography, published Manual PDF, California codes), that site has its own policy.

What we do not do

No ads. No sale of data. No tracking of other people on your phone.

Contact the developer listed on the App Store or Play listing with questions.
